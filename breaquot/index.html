Optics on the board

  • H
    Beam splitter: a branch that hits it splits in two. Half the probability bounces back, with its phase flipped (+ → −), and half passes through.
  • ▒
    Barrier: steel bounces the ball, but 15% of each branch tunnels through.
  • Z
    Phase plate: anything passing through gets its phase flipped. Cyan branches are |+⟩ and magenta ones are |−⟩.
  • ✕
    Interference: when two branches meet, they recombine. With the same phase, the merged ball keeps going their way. With opposite phases, it comes out the other way.
  • p
    Expected damage: a branch with probability p deals p damage, so splitting covers more bricks with weaker hits.
  • 〰
    Entangled bricks come in pairs. Break one and its partner shatters too.
  • 👁
    The paddle is a detector. A branch reaching the bottom is measured: with probability p, it's the real ball. Real and caught: it bounces. Real and missed: you lose a life. Not real: it vanishes, and its probability moves to the other branches.
  • ⌛
    Decoherence: a superposition lasts 12 s, then it collapses on its own. Space collapses it right away.
