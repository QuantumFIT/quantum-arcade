    Gates & effects

    • H
      Hadamard splits you into a superposition (|L⟩+|R⟩)/√2: two copies, mirrored across the maze. The mirror copy moves with mirrored controls, and both copies eat. A second H interferes you back, since H·H = I.
    • Z
      Phase flip takes ghosts from |+⟩ to |−⟩, and |−⟩ ghosts are edible. But Z flips |−⟩ back to |+⟩ too, so don't take two in a row.
    • ✕
      Interference: when a |+⟩ ghost and a |−⟩ ghost meet, they cancel out.
    • 〰
      Entanglement: ghosts come in Bell pairs. Eat one and its partner collapses too.
    • ?
      Schrödinger's Clyde is in two places at once until you observe him (line of sight).
    • ◎
      EPR portals: opposite corners are entangled.
    • ⇥
      Tunneling: go through a wall with probability e−0.6·w, where w is the wall thickness.
    • 👁
      Measurement: when a ghost touches one of your copies, the wavefunction collapses. With 50% chance you were never there.
