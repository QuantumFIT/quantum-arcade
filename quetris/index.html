Quantum rules

  • ⊕
    Superposed pieces are two shapes at once, e.g. (|T⟩+|S⟩)/√2. A move only works if both shapes fit. When either shape touches down, the piece is measured: 50/50 which one becomes real.
  • Z
    Phase flip (F) turns the piece into |−⟩ antimatter. A cell that lands on a cell of the opposite phase annihilates with it: |+⟩+|−⟩ → 0. Use it to dig out holes.
  • 〰
    Entangled pairs: two consecutive pieces share a Bell pair. Clear any of their cells and every other cell of the pair vanishes too.
  • 👁
    Measure (Q) collapses a superposed piece right now.
  • ⇣
    Tunneling (T): the piece passes through blocks to the next free spot below, with probability e−0.6·w for w rows of blocks.
  • +
    You get +1 Z flip and +1 tunnel every 5 cleared lines (5 at most).
